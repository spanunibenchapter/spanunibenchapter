About SPAN UNIBEN

Who we are, what we stand for and who belongs.

SPAN UNIBEN is the association of undergraduate Physiology students of the University of Benin. We exist to build a safe, united environment for intellectual, moral, socio-cultural and recreational growth, and to represent every Spanite before the University and the public.

Our aims

🤝 Represent

Protect and promote the interest and good image of the Association, and keep communication open with University authorities.

🌱 Develop

Create a safe space for intellectual, moral, socio-cultural and recreational development of members.

🕊️ Unite

Encourage peaceful coexistence among members and other institutions, regardless of origin, gender, politics or religion.

🏛️ Serve

Run an effective, accountable student body that keeps students, staff and the public in harmony.

Membership

Ordinary members

All registered undergraduate Physiology students at UNIBEN. Membership is validated by payment of dues. Members in good financial standing can attend activities, vote, and contest for office.

Life members

Graduates of the Department of Physiology. They use Association facilities, join social activities and advise members when called upon.

Honorary members & patrons

Persons approved by Parliament for their contribution to SPAN. The HOD and the Dean of Basic Medical Sciences are the statutory Grand Patrons.

Governance

How SPAN is structured under the 2021 Constitution.

The Executive Council

Elected by ordinary members. It runs programmes, protects Association property and reports to Parliament.

Parliament (SRC)

The legislative organ and highest policy-making body. It approves the budget, appoints the Audit Committee, summons officers and can impeach by a two-thirds vote. Led by the Speaker, Deputy Speaker, Clerk, Deputy Clerk and Chief Whip.

Committees

Audit

Reviews outgoing income and spending

Finance

Sole fundraising authority

Budget & Projects

Scrutinises budgets, monitors dues

Constitution Review

Interprets and reviews the constitution

Elections

Run by an independent Electoral Commission. Executive candidates need a CGPA of at least 2.5 and no more than 2 carryovers. Presidential candidates must be in 300 level or higher with prior SPAN experience.
